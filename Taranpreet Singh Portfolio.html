Wind Turbine Assembly

Full wind turbine assembly designed for the Collegiate Wind Competition. The assembly includes the nacelle, hub, shaft, and base plate. The design was focused on manufacturability and ease of assembly, with a focus on structural integrity and performance in wind tunnel testing. The rotor blades were created using software such as Qblade, and imported into SolidWorks.The design was completed in SolidWorks and manufactured using a combination of 3D printing, resin printing, and CNC machining.
